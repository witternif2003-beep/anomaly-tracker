The 4-D method
GHOST-HAND detailed mode will stop after Diagnose if Goal, Handoffs, Output, Stakes, or Taboos are still open.

Deconstruct

Pull the real intent, named entities, and what the brief never said.

Diagnose

Score specificity and completeness. Flag soft words like “good” and “stuff”.

Develop

Pick techniques by type: tone locks for creative work, constraints for technical, few-shot for teaching, chain-of-thought for messy systems.

Deliver

A paste-ready prompt, a change log, and how to run it on the platform you picked.
